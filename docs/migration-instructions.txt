The xaml pages are to be moved and renamed to axaml.

The Build Action for these muse be Avalonia XAML


Page Migration:

ContentView becomes utilities:ViewBase following this sample


Sample is found in ConnectPopupView with ConnectPopupViewModel

<utilities:ViewBase x:TypeArguments="connect:ConnectPopupViewModel"
                    xmlns="https://github.com/avaloniaui"
                    xmlns:x="http://schemas.microsoft.com/winfx/2006/xaml"
                    xmlns:d="http://schemas.microsoft.com/expression/blend/2008"
                    xmlns:mc="http://schemas.openxmlformats.org/markup-compatibility/2006"
                    xmlns:utilities="clr-namespace:MissionPlanner.AvaloniaUI.App.Utilities"
                    xmlns:connect="clr-namespace:MissionPlanner.AvaloniaUI.App.Views.Connect"
                    xmlns:avalonia="clr-namespace:Material.Icons.Avalonia;assembly=Material.Icons.Avalonia"
                    xmlns:u="https://irihi.tech/ursa"
                    mc:Ignorable="d" d:DesignWidth="800" d:DesignHeight="450"
                    x:Class="MissionPlanner.AvaloniaUI.App.Views.Connect.ConnectPopupView"
                    x:DataType="connect:ConnectPopupViewModel"
                    >


</utilities:ViewBase>

ViewModel Changes:
Baseclass for ViewModels changes from BaseViewModel to ViewModelBase
Use ViewModelBase that takes a ILogger as argument
remove local fielss logger and refer to Logger in ViewModelBase
Replace dispatcher.Dispatch...  with Dispatcher.Dispatch...





AXAML Changes:


For each axaml file, these changes must be applied:

HorizontalStackLayout becomes StackPanel with attribute Orientation="Horizontal" 
VerticalStackLayout becomes StackPanel with attribute Orientation="Vertical" 


These arguments must be changed

HorizontalOptions="Fill" becomes HorizontalAlignment="Stretch"
HorizontalOptions="Center" becomes HorizontalAlignment="Center"
HorizontalOptions="Start" becomes HorizontalAlignment="Left"
HorizontalOptions="End" becomes HorizontalAlignment="Right"


VerticalOptions="Fill" becomes VerticalAlignment="Stretch"
VerticalOptions="Center" becomes VerticalAlignment="Center"
VerticalOptions="Start" becomes VerticalAlignment="Top"
VerticalOptions="End" becomes VerticalAlignment="Bottom"




BackgroundColor becomes Background
TextColor becomes Foreground


In Label and Button controls, the Text attribute must be changed to Content


FontAttributes="Bold" becomes FontWeight="Bold"



		<material:SelectField 
                        ItemsSource="{Binding MapStyles}"
                        SelectedItem="{Binding SelectedMapStyle}" />

becomes


                <ComboBox 
                          ItemsSource="{Binding MapStyles}"
                          SelectedItem="{Binding SelectedMapStyle}"
                          DisplayMemberBinding="{Binding .}"
                          />


Text Fields 

	<controls:AlignedEditorField
	    Title="An Editor"
	    Text="Centered text"
	 />

becomes

	   <TextBox  PlaceholderText="An Editor" Text="Centered text"
           />



NumericField

                    <controls:NumericField
                        Value="{Binding StepSize, Mode=TwoWay}"
                        NumberFormat="F7"
			Min="0"
			Max="10000"                        
			StepSize="10" 
                        />

becomes:

            <NumericUpDown 
                    Value="{Binding StepSize, Mode=TwoWay}"

                   Minimum="0"
                   Maximum="10000"
                   Increment="10" />
